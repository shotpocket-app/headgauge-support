Common questions

What is free, and what does a subscription add?

Stage 1 is free. After stage 1, one free complete practice group is available each day: level 1 of any metric, or eye-height level 2 when unlocked. A subscription unlocks stages 2–4 and their checks, level 2 for mouth, nose and face width, all reviewed drawing and practice references, unlimited practice groups, and a personal plan after the route. Your saved records remain viewable without a subscription. See Apple’s purchase page in the app for the current price and renewal terms.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases in HeadGauge. The app checks your eligible subscription with Apple; restoring a purchase does not restore drawings or practice records.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In HeadGauge, open Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription when subscribed. You can also open your iPhone or iPad Settings → your Apple Account name → Subscriptions, then choose the subscription. Apple’s cancellation instructions explain the available options.

How do I change the language?

HeadGauge opens in English. You can switch to Simplified Chinese in Settings → Language; the choice is saved on this device. Apple’s purchase and system screens follow your device language. This website is in English only.

Where are my records stored?

Drawings and practice records stay on this device. There is no HeadGauge account or app-managed cloud sync. Deleting the app or losing the device can lose your records, and the developer cannot recover them. Purchase restoration does not restore local records.

How should I photograph a paper drawing?

Open Canvas → Photograph paper drawing and choose the reference you used. Lay the paper flat, keep the camera parallel to the page, use even lighting and keep all four corners visible. Select the correct paper size, adjust the corners, check the corrected image, then mark your drawing. These measurements guide practice; they do not establish camera or measurement accuracy.

Why do the results change when I mark again?

Point placement can vary between attempts, and the measurements are calculated from the points you mark. Marking again helps you see the difference between your placements. Keep the same eye mode and use visible landmarks rather than guessing hidden points.

Can I use Apple Pencil?

Yes. The sketch canvas supports Apple Pencil on compatible iPads. Pressure sensitivity is available only with Pencil models that support it; Apple Pencil (USB-C) uses the gray presets. Use two fingers to move and zoom the canvas.
