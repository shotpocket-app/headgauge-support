HeadGauge helps you practice judging portrait proportions. Measurements are practice guides calculated from the points you mark. They are not a judgment of your artwork, and they are not medical, identity or face recognition. All references are public-domain museum works, used for practice only. Your records stay on this device; deleting the app or losing the device loses them.
