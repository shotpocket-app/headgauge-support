Disclaimer

Updated November 23, 2026

HeadGauge helps you practice judging portrait proportions. Measurements are practice guides calculated from the points you mark. They are not a judgment of your artwork, and they are not medical, identity or face recognition. The reference portraits supplied by HeadGauge are public-domain museum works for drawing practice. Records are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app or losing the device may result in data loss; the developer cannot recover your records. Device backups may include app data depending on your system settings. HeadGauge does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service.
