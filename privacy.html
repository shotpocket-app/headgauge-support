HeadGauge / Privacy

Privacy policy

Updated November 23, 2026

HeadGauge for iPhone and iPad is now available on the App Store. Download the app from the App Store; purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple in the app.

This policy covers the HeadGauge app and this support website. HeadGauge is developed by the independent developer using the GitHub account shotpocket-app. Contact: shotpocket6@gmail.com.

Your drawings and practice records are stored locally on your device. Optional support emails and visits to this website are separate activities described below.

1. Information stored on your device

HeadGauge stores your drawings, reference photos you choose, corrected copies of paper drawings, marked points, measurement and practice records, and route progress on your device. It also stores a random ID used in optional feedback summaries. The developer does not automatically receive this information.

2. Camera and photo access

Camera access is used only when you choose to photograph a paper drawing. Apple’s photo picker provides only the image you select. When you choose a personal photo as a portrait reference, HeadGauge saves a local JPEG copy with your work. When you photograph or import a paper drawing, HeadGauge saves the corrected image. Image processing and measurement happen on your device. The app does not automatically send either kind of image to the developer.

3. Accounts, servers and tracking

HeadGauge has no app account, developer-operated server, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not use remote AI processing. Drawings, marked points and practice records are processed locally.

4. Optional feedback

From a result page, you can prepare a local feedback summary containing the same locally generated random ID across reports, app and rule versions, task and stage times, complete practice groups, drawing input types, marking time, first repeat differences, and whether you know your next stroke. It contains no photos, drawings or landmark coordinates. The judgment-accuracy answer stays on the device.

Result feedback opens a mail composer with the summary in the message and a JSON summary attachment. Feedback from Settings includes only the app version and device model, with no practice summary or images. Both are addressed to shotpocket6@gmail.com. You review the email and decide whether to send it. If you send a result summary, the developer receives the random ID in the same message as your email address.

5. Subscriptions

Apple handles purchases and restoration. HeadGauge checks whether your subscription is active on the device; the developer does not receive your payment details through the app. Purchase or restoration may require an internet connection. Restoring a subscription does not restore drawings or practice records. This website does not sell subscriptions or process payments.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records are stored on this device. Deleting the app or losing the device may result in data loss. Device backups may include app data depending on your system settings; these backups are separate from HeadGauge, which does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service. The developer cannot remotely read, recover or delete content stored on your device. Deleting the app does not delete an email you already sent or the original image in your Photos library.

7. Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and any message or attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond and investigate the issue. Email providers, including Gmail for this inbox, process the correspondence. Do not send other people’s photos, passwords or payment information.

Correspondence is retained while needed for the request and related follow-up, or where required by law. To request deletion of support correspondence, email shotpocket6@gmail.com.

8. This website

These static pages add no analytics, advertising, forms, cookies or local-storage tracking. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security and may process other technical service data under the GitHub Privacy Statement. When you follow an email or external link, the service you choose applies its own privacy practices.

9. Intended users and updates

HeadGauge is for people practicing portrait drawing and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly solicit children’s information through support. Contact us if you believe such information was sent. We will update this policy if data practices change; the date above identifies this version.
